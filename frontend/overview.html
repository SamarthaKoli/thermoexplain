What is CFD?

Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) uses numerical Navier-Stokes approximations to solve how cooling fluids move and exchange heat around cells over time.

Coolant Channel Geometry

The narrow 2.5 mm conduits separating cells through which water-glycol is pumped to absorb heat dissipation.

Reynolds Number (Re) Physics

Dimensionless ratio of inertial to viscous forces. Low Re (≤2300) represents laminar flow; high Re (>4000) represents swirling turbulent convection.

Temperature Uniformity ΔT Target

Keeping cell-to-cell spread within <5°C. Uneven cooling causes current mismatch and early module degradation.
