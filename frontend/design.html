What do these numbers tell us?

The total temperature spread is ΔT_max = 17.6 °C. In automotive production battery packs, engineering standards recommend keeping the inter-cell temperature differential below 5.0 °C. A 17.6 °C spread under low laminar flow (Re=560) can accelerate capacity degradation and premature cell aging in downstream cells #7 through #10.
