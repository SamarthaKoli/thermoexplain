Why do we evaluate?

A working model is not necessarily an accurate model. Evaluation compares predictions or generated outputs with appropriate reference data and tests whether results can be trusted within a defined scope. In safety-critical battery pack cooling, statistical alignment without thermodynamic constraint validation introduces high systemic risk.

Comparing SHAP and LIME in Battery CFD:

SHAP calculates cooperative game-theoretic Shapley contributions across all feature permutations, while LIME constructs an interpretable linear surrogate in the local neighborhood. When both methods agree on primary drivers (U_in and Q_cell), researcher confidence increases, but neither method guarantees physical causal mechanisms or Navier-Stokes compliance.
